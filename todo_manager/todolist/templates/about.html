About us

Building tools that
make life simpler

TodoManager is a clean, fast, and intuitive task management app built with Django. No fluff — just what you need to get things done.

Our mission

We believe productivity should be simple. TodoManager was created to help individuals and teams organise their work without the complexity of bloated tools. Every feature is intentional, every interaction is smooth, and your data stays yours.

Fast by design

Built with Django and PostgreSQL, every action completes instantly. No waiting, no reloads — just seamless task management.

Privacy first

Your tasks are stored securely and never shared. No tracking, no ads, no third-party access.

Made with care

Every pixel and interaction is crafted thoughtfully. We care about the details that make your experience better.
